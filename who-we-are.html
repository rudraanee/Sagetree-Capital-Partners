Who We Are

We sit exclusively on
your side of the table.

Built on principle. Grown with purpose.

Sagetree Capital is a regulated external asset manager managing multiple family offices, providing independent, conflict-free wealth management for enterprising families, entrepreneurs and their holding structures. We work exclusively with family offices, serving a limited number of clients with the depth and continuity of a single-family office while sharing infrastructure and investment capabilities across families.

Our role is to design and oversee the family's total investment architecture, coordinate best-in-class specialists, and ensure consistent implementation of the agreed strategy across banks, custodians and jurisdictions.

We believe long-term wealth creation comes from a clear plan, structured risk management and the prudence to ensure permanent capital safety.
